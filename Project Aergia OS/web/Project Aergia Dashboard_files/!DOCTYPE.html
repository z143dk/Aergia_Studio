Agentic AI Trading Blueprint

Vision & Current Status

The overarching goal is to develop an agentic AI capable of consistently generating profits in financial markets. The project started with a crucial learning period focusing on **swing trading equity options** with an initial capital of **$500**, aiming for an incredibly ambitious **100% growth every other day** to achieve our target of growing the account past **$50,000**. This phase emphasized building a capital-efficient and risk-averse system under severe constraints.

The immediate next phase involves securing significant capital ($50,000+) and transitioning to multi-asset day trading strategies to achieve rapid and sustainable capital growth.

Agent Core Components

Data Ingestion & Preprocessing

Pulls market data, news, sentiment. Preprocesses for analysis.

NLP & Sentiment Analysis (Gemini)

Quantifies news/social media sentiment, identifies rumors. Powered by Gemini.

Signal Generation (ML Models)

Generates trading signals and predictions using XGBoost, LSTMs, etc.

Option Pricing & Greeks

Calculates theoretical option prices and sensitivities (Delta, Vega, Theta).

Risk Management (Crucial!)

Monitors portfolio risk, dynamically sizes positions, sets intelligent stop-losses, and implements circuit breakers.

Execution & Order Management

Low-latency order placement via brokerage APIs (Schwab, IBKR, Crypto).

LLM (Gemini) - Trading Coach & Adaptability

The **Gemini LLM** is crucial for the agent's continuous learning and rapid adaptation, especially with the ambitious growth target. It performs:

  • **Real-time Post-Trade Analysis:** Ingests trade outcomes and provides human-readable explanations of success/failure.
  • **Meta-Learning for Agents:** Identifies patterns to refine other agent parameters (e.g., sentiment misinterpretations).
  • **Strategy Adaptation & Generation:** Suggests new strategies or refines existing ones based on market shifts.
  • **Handling Unforeseen Events:** Analyzes unique market situations and recommends adaptive measures.
