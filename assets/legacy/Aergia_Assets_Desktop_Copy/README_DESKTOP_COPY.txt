Aergia Assets Desktop Copy

How to use:
1) Download and unzip this folder onto your Windows Desktop.
2) Copy the contents into your project folder:
   C:\Users\z143d\Aergia_Studio\assets\
   - banners\
   - icons\
   - wallpapers\
3) Use MANIFEST.csv to see what went where.

Notes:
- Folders are numbered to match the Pantheon order.
- Variants (multiple banners/portraits) are kept together under each god.
- Anything not matched goes into Extras_Raw.
