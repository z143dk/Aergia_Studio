[INSTRUCTIONS FOR iPHONE / iPAD]

1. Open the "Files" app on your iPhone/iPad.
2. Go to iCloud Drive -> Desktop -> Aergia_Studio -> _MOBILE_INSTALL_HELPER
3. Tap the file named "TAP_ME_TO_INSTALL.html"
4. It will open Safari and try to connect to your Mac.

[IMPORTANT]
- Your Mac must be turned ON and AWAKE.
- If you close your MacBook lid, it will go to sleep and disconnect the phone (unless you are plugged into a monitor + power).
- Aergia Studio must be running on your Mac.
- Your phone must be on the SAME WiFi as your Mac.
